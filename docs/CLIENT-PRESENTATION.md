# Client presentation

BrightonLive should be presented as a useful local product first.

## One sentence

**BrightonLive brings places, what's on, travel and local businesses together on one clear
Brighton & Hove map.**

## The difference

The local-data pipeline is conservative without turning the public website into a compliance
dashboard:

- discovery is broad;
- publication is narrow;
- sources are retained;
- weak/conflicting records go to review;
- direct business/organiser links remain prominent.

For an early business listing, appropriate facts supplied by the business can remain recorded as
business-supplied provenance, then be validated and versioned through subsequent changes.

"Source checked" means the information has passed the BrightonLive evidence process. It is not an
endorsement or quality guarantee.
