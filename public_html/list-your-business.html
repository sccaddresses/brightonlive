For local businesses

Be part of BrightonLive from the beginning.

This is the page prospective clients need: what BrightonLive is building, what a useful listing contains and how a Brighton & Hove business can register interest.

The proposition

Local discovery without the directory sludge.

BrightonLive is intended to sit between a map, an events guide and a local directory. A business can be found because it is relevant to the place, neighbourhood, event or journey — not because it paid to shout louder than everyone else.

What your listing can include

Name · category · address · map point · contact · opening information · direct website/booking links · social links · accessibility notes · approved imagery · relevant events.

Founding listing enquiry

Send the essentials.

You do not need to write marketing copy. Plain, accurate information is more useful.

  • Business or organisation name
  • Full address and postcode
  • Website and public contact details
  • Opening information or booking URL
  • One or two sentences on what you actually do
  • Accessibility information where relevant
  • Approved images or official social links
Email hello@brightonlive.uk

Submitting information is a request for consideration and does not guarantee publication, placement or ranking.

Why now

The useful time to join is before the directory is crowded.

Founding businesses can help BrightonLive understand which fields, links and local details matter before the public directory becomes large.
