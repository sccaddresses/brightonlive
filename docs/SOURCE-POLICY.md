# Source policy

Preferred evidence order:

1. organisation-owned primary page / structured data;
2. relevant authoritative public register;
3. official destination/council/venue source;
4. other independent corroborating source;
5. community/open data for discovery;
6. search/social lead requiring review.

Social posts and search results may discover an event or business, but discovery provenance does
not automatically give permission or sufficient evidence for automated publication.

Source probes record availability; a successful HTTP response does not itself make the source
publication-authoritative.
